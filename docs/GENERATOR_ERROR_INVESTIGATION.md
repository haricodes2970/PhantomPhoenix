## 1. Executive Summary

This report investigates generator-specific false negatives across the 470 AI-generated test samples in our 6,000-image research dataset. In particular, we analyze why the frozen detector (`buildborderless/CommunityForensics-DeepfakeDet-ViT` @ revision `ac6ee457...`) exhibits extreme variance in recall across generator families, most notably a severe recall collapse on `flux.2-klein-9b` (recall = 4.71% at threshold 0.20).

Key findings:
- **Score Collapse on FLUX:** The 85 test images generated by `flux.2-klein-9b` yield a mean fake probability score of **0.0189** (max 0.2403), placing them firmly inside the natural real-image distribution. The detector's feature space treats FLUX outputs as indistinguishable from genuine photographs.
- **Architecture & Training Gap:** The Community Forensics ViT detector was trained primarily on older GAN and early diffusion architectures (ProGAN, early SD 1.5/2.1). Modern rectified-flow / flow-matching architectures (FLUX) and highly polished turbo distillations (`z-image-turbo` mean score = 0.2518) lack the high-frequency upsampling artifacts or spectral grid anomalies that the detector's attention layers rely on.
- **High-Performance Families:** Conversely, stylized/anime models (`illustrious`, mean score = 0.8063) and video-derived frames (`wan-video-2.5`, mean score = 0.6674) retain detectable artifacts that yield high recall (86.89% and 76.92% respectively).

## 3. Preprocessing and Model-Identity Checks
- **Model Identity:** `buildborderless/CommunityForensics-DeepfakeDet-ViT` @ revision `ac6ee457...`.
- **Preprocessing:** `ViTImageProcessor` with shortest edge 440, center crop 384, and CLIP normalization.
- **Attention Implementation:** `eager` attention.
- **Conclusion:** Preprocessing and model loading are verified to match specifications exactly. The recall failures on FLUX and Sora are not implementation bugs; they are genuine zero-shot out-of-distribution transfer failures on modern generative architectures.

---

## 4. Confounders and Alternative Explanations
- **Resolution/Format Confounding:** While format-only classifiers can exploit resolution differences (Experiment E2), generator-specific recall failure cannot be explained by resolution alone because both high-recall families (`illustrious`) and low-recall families (`flux`) share standard latent resolutions (e.g. 1024x1024).
- **Architectural Shift:** FLUX utilizes rectified flow matching with double-stream blocks, differing fundamentally from the CNN/GAN and early DiT architectures the detector was trained on.

---

## 5. Limitations and Unresolved Questions
- **Minor Family Uncertainty:** Families with n < 10 (`sora-2`, `gpt-image-2`) have insufficient support for definitive family-level claims.
- **Fine-Tuning Absence:** Because no training or fine-tuning is performed in this sprint, fixing this recall gap requires future model fine-tuning or secondary ensemble integration.
